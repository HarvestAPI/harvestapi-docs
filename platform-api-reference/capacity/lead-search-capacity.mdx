title: 'Lead Search Capacity'
openapi: 'GET /linkedin/lead-search-capacity'
---

Shows how much of the general system capacity for [Search Leads](/linkedin-api-reference/leads/search-leads),
[Search Accounts](/linkedin-api-reference/accounts/search-accounts) and
[Account Insights](/linkedin-api-reference/accounts/account-insights) is already used in the current
hour and in the current day (UTC), in percent.

`100` means nothing is left for the rest of the hour (`hour`) or of the day (`day`), and requests
to these endpoints can fail until capacity is available again.
